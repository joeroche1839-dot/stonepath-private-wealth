Our Story

Built on discipline & the long game

StonePath Private Wealth Management was founded by David Roche, a Lindsay, Ontario native who played professional hockey in the NHL before turning his focus to helping families build their financial futures.

Drafted by the Pittsburgh Penguins in 1993, David went on to play in the NHL with Pittsburgh, the Calgary Flames and the New York Islanders, and captured a Calder Cup championship with the Saint John Flames. A career at the highest level taught him what it takes to succeed over the long run: preparation, teamwork, patience and the discipline to stick to the plan when it matters most.

Today David lives in Cavan and works as a financial advisor in the Peterborough area, where he's also active in the community coaching minor hockey. He brings that same long-game mindset to StonePath — sitting down with each client personally, building a clear plan, and staying in their corner for the seasons ahead.
